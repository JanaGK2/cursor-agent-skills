# Contributing

Contributions welcome. Please open an issue first to discuss new skills or significant changes before building them — this avoids duplicated effort and ensures the addition fits the collection.

**For new skills:**
- Follow the structure of existing skills: a `SKILL.md` at minimum, optionally `templates/`, `scripts/`, `reference/`
- Include attribution if your skill draws on third-party documentation (Google, Anthropic, etc.)
- Skills should be practical and tested — include real usage examples where possible

**For improvements to existing skills:**
- Open an issue describing what you want to change and why
- Keep pull requests focused on one change at a time

**License:** By submitting a pull request, you agree your contribution will be under the [MIT License](LICENSE).
